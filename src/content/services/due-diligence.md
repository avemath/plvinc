Transaction support for acquisitions, divestitures, and financings, delivered on the deal timeline.

Due diligence support includes:

- Leasehold title review and net acreage verification
- Lease term analysis (primary term status, continuous drilling provisions, HBP confirmation)
- Division order and royalty obligation review
- Surface use agreement and ROW inventory
- Identification of title defects, outstanding obligations, and encumbrances
- Net revenue interest (NRI) and working interest (WI) verification
- Consent-to-assign (CTA) and preferential right to purchase (pref right) review
- Estoppel certificate coordination
- Data room review and summary reporting
- Coordination with counsel, engineers, and transaction teams

Findings are delivered as organized summary reporting for decision-makers and transaction teams.

Leasehold acquisition from owner identification through executed and recorded lease.

Services include:

- Mineral and surface owner identification and contact
- Oil, gas, and mineral lease negotiation and execution
- Lease analysis and term verification (primary term, royalty, depth clauses, Pugh clauses)
- Pooling and unitization assistance
- Lease extension, ratification, and amendment
- Top leasing and lease maintenance programs
- Bulk leasehold acquisition coordination

Bonus and royalty calculations, legal descriptions, execution, and recording are verified at each step.

Shannon's work covers title examination, lease acquisition, mineral and royalty management, and land administration, work where the details decide whether a project is sound.

She handles a project from start to finish: title research and ownership checks, lease negotiation and signing, division order review, and ongoing royalty and mineral records. She has worked on projects across the Gulf Coast, including East Texas and Louisiana, and her files are documented and organized so that anyone checking the work can follow it.

Clients with complicated ownership, many interests to track, or a deal on a deadline rely on her for accurate records and clear answers.

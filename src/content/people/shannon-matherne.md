Shannon Matherne, CPL, brings focused professional expertise in title examination, lease acquisition, mineral and royalty management, and land administration — work that demands precision, meticulous organization, and close attention to the details that determine whether a project is sound.

Shannon holds the Certified Professional Landman designation and works across the full project lifecycle: initial title research and ownership verification, lease negotiation and execution, division order review and analysis, and ongoing royalty and mineral portfolio management. She has worked on projects across the Gulf Coast, East Texas, and Louisiana plays, and is known for well-documented, tightly organized work product that holds up under scrutiny at every stage.

Her commitment to accuracy and clear communication makes her a trusted resource for clients managing complex ownership structures, multi-interest portfolios, and time-sensitive transactions.

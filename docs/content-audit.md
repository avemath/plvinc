# Content audit, plvinc.com

Audited 9 November 2026 on the local build of the site as it stood after the price bar change, read top to bottom as a first-time visitor would, at 1440px and 375px wide.

How to read the numbers:

- "Starts at" is how far down the page a section begins, in screen-heights. One screen is 900px tall at 1440 wide (a laptop) and 812px tall at 375 wide (a phone). So "2.3" means the visitor has to scroll past more than two full screens to reach it.
- Word counts are approximate and count everything visible in the section, including button text.
- Contact prompts count the buttons and links on the page that call, email or go to the contact form. The header "Get in touch" button, the header phone, the footer phone and email, and the phone bar on mobile appear on every page and are not counted below.

## 2. Repeated content

### Useful repeats (keep: a visitor landing from Google needs these on whatever page they land on)

- **Who You'll Talk To** (Home, Mineral Owners, Companies): faces and names are the strongest trust signal for a two-person firm. Keep, but compact on the inner pages.
- **CPL credential** in some form on every page: keep.
- **Sample file teaser** (Home, Services, Mineral Owners, Companies, Experience, four service pages): one short line and a link is enough on inner pages; the full teaser block with the paper graphic is only needed on Home.
- **"What will it cost?"** answered on both Mineral Owners and Companies: each audience needs it, and the two answers are already written for their reader.
- **Conflict disclosure** (Mineral Owners and Companies): required by the AAPL Code of Ethics and written for each audience.

### Filler repeats (cut or compact)

- **What Happens When You Contact Us**, four steps, near word for word on Home, Mineral Owners and Contact. One full version (Contact) plus a one-row strip elsewhere is enough.
- **"A CPL on every project" / "No guessing"** appear word for word as the Home "why us" points and inside the Companies intro.
- **At a glance cards** on Mineral Owners, Companies, Experience and Sample Work repeat the same four facts (CPL, since 2005, 8 states, Duncansville) that the Home strip already shows.
- **Where We've Worked state cards**: the same eight cards on Companies and on Experience, plus the map on Home.
- **"From curative work on a single tract to multi-county leasing programs..."** appears five times: Home services intro, Companies intro, Companies FAQ, About intro and Rickey's bio.
- **"We take work in any state"** appears six times across Home, Companies (twice), Experience (twice) and the Companies FAQ.
- **"We run title all the way to the end of the chain, not just to the first clean deed"** and **"written so you can hand it straight to an attorney, a lender or a deal team"** both appear on Companies and on About.

## 5. Copy problems

### Pointing visitors elsewhere first

- Mineral Owners, At a glance: "we are glad to work alongside your attorney."
- Mineral Owners, bottom note: "Many owners keep their own attorney involved, and we are glad to work alongside one." It is the last thing before the closing call, so the page ends on another professional instead of on Rickey and Shannon.
- Mineral Owners, conflict note: "we will not review it for you" is true and must stay, but it reads as a refusal. It can say the same thing and still ask the owner to call.
- Services page, owner note: "This page is written for the companies we work for." It sends owners to their own page, which is fine, but it reads as "not for you" rather than "here is the page for you".
- The site-wide footer line "We are landmen, not attorneys, and nothing on this site is legal advice" is a required disclaimer and is fine as it is.

### Weak or generic lines

- Services closing: "Describe the project and we'll talk through how to approach it. No obligation." Generic, and almost the same as the Companies closing ("Have a project? Send us the scope.").
- Experience closing: "Have similar work coming up? Let's talk." Says nothing about why.
- Mineral Owners FAQ, "Is the offer a fair price?": the answer never says what they do for the owner in one short line. It could lead with what the owner gets.
- Service cards: "Learn more" on all six. Fine, but it is the only template-feel link on the site.
- Experience, Project Types: eight bare labels with no detail or example.

### Facts that disagree

- **Years of experience.** Home and Experience say "45 combined years in oil and gas" and "each of us has more than 20 years". Rickey's bio says hands-on land work since 1997, which is 29 years, so with Shannon over 20 the total is at least 49. Needs the client to confirm the right figure. Shannon's bio gives no start year at all.
- **Where the firm works, on the owners page.** Mineral Owners is written for Pennsylvania only (page title "Oil & Gas Lease Offers in Pennsylvania", "Work across central and western Pennsylvania", the county list), while every other page says files in 8 states and work in any state. Rickey's bio says much of his work is in East Texas and Louisiana. An owner in Texas, Louisiana or Ohio lands on a page that seems not to be for them.
- **Basins in search descriptions.** The Title Research, Due Diligence and Mineral & Royalty search descriptions name only the Appalachian and Gulf Coast basins; Home and Contact name five regions.
- Phone, email, CPL and Duncansville are consistent everywhere.

### Jargon a landowner would not know

Explained already: division order, decimal, chain of title, runsheet (on Home and Sample Work), A&D, HBP, forced pooling, bonus, Pugh clause, suspense.

Not explained on the owner-facing pages: net mineral acre (in "Bonus per acre"), unitization, shut-in royalty, Recorder of Deeds, Register of Wills and Orphans' Court, curative (on Home tiles), basin and play (on the Home map), greenfield (Experience).

## 6. Visual problems

- **Walls of text:** Mineral Owners FAQ (524 words, all answers open) and lease offer section (582 words, nine terms in one long list); Companies FAQ (270 words) just above the closing.
- **About looks unfinished:** the "How We Work" box is followed by a loose paragraph ("We work for operators...") that reads like it belongs above it; the Affiliations band holds one item; the Credentials list is empty and hidden.
- **Experience Project Types:** a thin two-column list of short labels in a lot of white space, the most template-like section on the site.
- **Service page sidebars are uneven:** four of six services have no "What you get", none has "Who this is for", so some sidebars are full and some hold only the phone.
- **Mineral Owners Pennsylvania section:** plain bullets under a heading with no label or framing, followed by small grey notes; it looks like an appendix.
- **Spacing:** Mineral Owners and Companies alternate cream and white bands for 10 or more sections, so the rhythm becomes monotonous and nothing stands out as the main content.

## 7. Top 10 improvements, highest impact first

1. **Mineral Owners: lead with what only this page has.** Lease offer, key terms, Pennsylvania and FAQ start 4.7 to 9.9 screens down (8 to 17 on a phone) behind sections that repeat Home.
2. **Mineral Owners: speak to owners outside Pennsylvania.** The firm has files in 8 states and Rickey works mostly in Texas and Louisiana, but the owners page reads as Pennsylvania only, so out-of-state owners leave.
3. **Home, Experience: confirm "45 combined years".** Rickey's bio (since 1997) plus Shannon's 20+ adds up to at least 49; a wrong number on the first screen undercuts the trust it is meant to build.
4. **Mineral Owners: stop pointing owners to an attorney.** Two lines suggest an attorney before Rickey and Shannon have spoken to the owner, and one of them sits right before the closing call.
5. **Companies: bring the FAQ up and cut prompts to 3 or 4.** Timeline, cost and who-runs-it answers are what a land manager checks first, and they sit 6 screens down with 5 prompts above them.
6. **Sample Work: surface "What Makes a File Hard".** It is the best sales copy on the site, at 8.6 screens (16.6 on a phone); a short version near the top here, or on Companies, would carry it.
7. **Site-wide: compact the filler repeats.** Make the four-step process, the "CPL on every project" points, the At a glance cards and the state grid one full version each, with compact strips elsewhere.
8. **About: finish the page.** Move the "who we work for" paragraph above How We Work, give Shannon's bio a start year and add credentials or memberships so the bands are not one item each (needs facts from the client).
9. **Experience: give Project Types substance.** One line per type, or examples by state, turns a bare label list into proof of work (needs facts from the client).
10. **Service pages: fill "What you get" and "Who this is for" in /studio.** Four services lack the first and all six lack the second, so the sidebars look uneven and the pages sell less than Title and Lease.
